Veneto

Classificazione geograficaSettled by ancient Celtic people (Veneti) in 1st mill. BCE; ruled by Rome from 2nd cen. BCE; invaded by Huns in mid-5th cen. CE; area gained financial and military prominence in 15th cen.; was passed between France and Austria during 18th-19th cen.
