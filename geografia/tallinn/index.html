Tallinn

Descrizione

Fortified settlement existed on site from late 1st mill. BCE; town flourished by 12th cen. CE; captured by Danes in 1219, new fortress built on Toompea hill; ruled by Teutonic Knights from 1346, by Sweden from 1561, & by Russia from 1710.
