Emilia-Romagna

Classificazione geograficaRich agricultural area bordering Adriatic Sea; Emilia and Romagna fell to Lombards 5th century CE; Emilia went to Franks 8th century, duchy of Modena by 17th century, then to Parma and Piacenza; Romagna was ruled by Byzantines 6th century, and by Pope from 16th century.
