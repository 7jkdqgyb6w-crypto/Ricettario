Toscana

Area was core of ancient Etruria; conquered by Romans 4th century BCE; ruled by Lombards, then Franks; most cities became independent communes 11th-12th centuries; saw great strife between Guelphs and Ghibellines; part of grand duchy of Florence from the 16th century.
