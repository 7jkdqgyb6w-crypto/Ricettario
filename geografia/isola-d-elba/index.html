Isola d'Elba

Descrizione

Largest Italian island after Sicily and Sardinia; it is the largest remaining land mass that once connected the Italian peninsula to Corsica. Iron ore has been mined here since Antiquity; was conquered by Etruscans, later by Romans (453 BCE); invaded by Longobards; dominated by Greek, Norman and Saracen pirates (9th-10th centuries CE).
