Umbria

Classificazione geograficaWas home of ancient Umbri tribes, who spoke an Indo-European dialect and were concentrated here to escape Etruscan and Gallic pressure during 1st mill. BCE; later held by Romans, Lombards and Byzantines; city-states developed; went to Papal States in the 16th century.
