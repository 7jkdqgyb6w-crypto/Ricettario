Croazia

Area was part of ancient Pannonia; settled by Slavic Croats from Ukraine 7th century CE; formed kingdom in 10th century; ruled by Hungary 1091-1526, then by Turks until late 17th century; was part of Austria-Hungary; was part of Yugoslavia from 1929; official language is Croatian.
